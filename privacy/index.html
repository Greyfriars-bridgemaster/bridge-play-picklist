Privacy, cookies and accessibility

Who runs this site

This site is run by Greyfriars Bridge Club, an unincorporated association, for the students of its bridge school. To contact the club, use the details on the club's website.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and we do not ask for or keep any information about you.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, delivers the pages to you. Like any web host, it handles technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the site, keep it secure and keep it running. We do not use this information ourselves. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies

The student pages do not set cookies or store anything on your device, and they do not load anything from Google or other outside companies, so there is no cookie banner.

The sign-in page used by the site's maintainers keeps the maintenance password in the browser for the length of the visit, so that the page can work. It is cleared when the browser is closed.

Boards you load into Play the Hand

If you load your own board file into Play the Hand, it is read inside your browser only. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

Other websites

Links to other sites, such as the club's BridgeWebs site and the map showing the Centre's address, lead to services run by others, with their own privacy and cookie notices.

Your rights and complaints

If you have a question about how your information is handled, please contact the club. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Accessibility

We have aimed for large, clear text, strong colour contrast, and pages that can be used with a keyboard and on a phone. If you find something hard to read or use, please tell the club through the website above and we will try to put it right.
